# State income tax audit

Reviewed November 20, 2026. Covers **47 states** for a single, full-year resident with no dependents, excluding California, Massachusetts, and New York as requested. The audit compares the app's current rules with resident schedules, statutes, and official estimated-tax instructions. It covers ordinary state income tax and major exceptions relevant to the app's inputs. Local taxes, payroll charges, tax-table rounding, and eligibility for specialized credits can change a particular return.

The bracket entries below show the lower boundary of each marginal band as `taxable income → rate`. A flat rate starts at zero unless a zero-rate band is shown. Dollar amounts are annual. When only one schedule appears in the 2025–2026 column, it applies to both years unless the row identifies an open item. Estimates and open items are identified separately from confirmed legislation.

## Implementation status

The table records findings against the app at the time of the audit. The calculator and comparison now use the published 2027 amounts for Maine, Montana, Nebraska, North Carolina, and Virginia. The implementation also updates the 2025 Arkansas regular bands and low-income table, Alabama's standard-deduction steps, Colorado's strict high-income threshold, the Maine and Minnesota phaseouts, the federal itemization election for Oklahoma, Rhode Island's phaseout, Utah's credit thresholds, Vermont's rate schedules and 2025 deductions, and both Wisconsin corrections.

Idaho's 2026 zero-rate cutoff, Oregon's 2026 deduction and credit, and Vermont's 2026 deduction and exemption retain their earlier estimates while final return figures are unresolved. Maine's 2027 bands, surcharge, standard deduction, and personal exemption are published. Its separate phaseout thresholds use 2026 amounts pending confirmation. These combinations are marked provisional in Calculator and Compare. The audit's eligibility and local-tax coverage limits still apply.
